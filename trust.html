Confidentiality and ethics

Trust matters when a student shares personal writing.

The review process is designed to protect student privacy, preserve student voice, and keep the work aligned with application integrity.

Private Student Work

Essays, prompts, deadlines, and student details are handled as confidential review materials and are not shared publicly.

Student Voice First

Feedback is intended to help the student make stronger choices. The final essay should still sound like the student.

No Ghostwriting

The service provides coaching, comments, and editorial direction. It does not replace the student's authorship or write the application essay for them.

AI-Aware Review

Feedback can help remove generic or template-like language and bring the essay back to concrete lived experience and reflection.

Admissions Integrity

Families are responsible for following each college, scholarship, or application platform's policies on outside feedback and originality. College Essay Incubator supports ethical revision, not misrepresentation.
