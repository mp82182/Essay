From the Essay Incubator method

Great essays usually start before the first sentence.

The review looks for the raw material students often overlook: the recurring interests, small moments, contradictions, and opening hooks that can make an application essay feel alive.

01

Choose the idea readers remember

Many drafts are well-written but forgettable. The first job is finding the story with the clearest emotional and admissions value.

02

Look for the best material you left out

Students often skip the details that reveal personality. The review helps identify what belongs back in the essay.

03

Use specific interests as evidence

An obsession, habit, side project, or repeated question can become a doorway into values, curiosity, and intellectual energy.

04
