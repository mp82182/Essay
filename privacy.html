Privacy policy

How student essays and family information are handled.

College Essay Incubator collects only the information needed to review an essay, communicate with the student or parent, and process payment.

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Information Collected

When you submit a form, College Essay Incubator may collect the student's name, parent or student email, application deadline, target school or application context, notes, selected review option, and uploaded essay file.

How Information Is Used

  • To review the submitted essay and provide coaching or editorial feedback.
  • To communicate about deadlines, payment, scheduling, and follow-up questions.
  • To maintain basic business records for completed services.

Student Privacy

Student essays and personal information are treated as confidential. Essays are not sold, published, or shared for marketing without explicit permission.

Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services for form submissions, file upload, payment processing, hosting, or analytics. These providers process information according to their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the Contact page.
