Frequently asked questions

Everything families need to know before submitting.

Here are the key details about fit, review options, payment flow, confidentiality, and how the feedback protects the student's own voice.

Still deciding?

If you are unsure which review option fits your deadline or essay stage, send a quick note first.

What should I include when I submit?

Include the essay draft, prompt, target school or application type, deadline, selected review option, and any specific concerns you want addressed.

Can I ask if this is a fit before paying?

Yes. Send the essay stage, deadline, and goal first if you are not sure which option makes sense. Families should feel clear on the next step before paying.

What is included in the $975 review?

The $975 option includes one admissions-focused read, written comments, revision priorities, and the option of a live call or private voice feedback link.

What is included in the $1,500 review?

The $1,500 option includes two rounds of review, so the student can revise after the first round and receive a second expert pass for polish and final confidence.

How is feedback delivered?

Feedback is personalized and can be paired with a live call or a private voice feedback link so the student understands what to revise and why. The goal is a clear revision path, not vague encouragement.

Will the essay still sound like the student?

Yes. This is coaching and editorial feedback, not ghostwriting. The review helps the student make stronger choices while preserving their authentic voice.

Can this help if I used AI while brainstorming?

Yes, as long as the final essay is the student's own work. The review helps remove generic, template-like language and bring the essay back to specific lived experience, voice, and reflection.

What makes an essay memorable?

Memorable essays usually have a specific idea, a strong opening, vivid personal details, and reflection that shows how the student thinks. The review helps find and sharpen those pieces.

Are essays confidential?

Yes. Essays and student details are treated privately and are never shared.

How does payment work?

Payment is currently set up for PayPal checkout links. The live PayPal URLs still need to be inserted before publishing so customers can pay securely.

Can the site upload essays directly?

The site is now structured for direct file upload through a form endpoint. Before launch, connect the form to a service such as Formspree, Basin, Getform, Tally, or a custom secure upload endpoint.
