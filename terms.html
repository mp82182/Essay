Terms and refund policy

Clear expectations before a family submits an essay.

These terms explain what the service provides, how payment works, and how student authorship is protected.

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Service

College Essay Incubator provides coaching and editorial feedback on college application essays. Feedback may include comments on story, structure, clarity, voice, opening hooks, reflection, and revision priorities.

No Admissions Guarantee

Essay feedback can improve clarity and presentation, but it cannot guarantee admission, scholarships, interview invitations, or application outcomes.

Student Authorship

The service does not provide ghostwriting. The student remains responsible for the final essay and for following each college or application platform's rules on originality and outside assistance.

Payment And Refunds

Payment is due before review work begins. Because time is reserved for each review, refunds are available only before review work has started unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Turnaround

Turnaround timing depends on the package selected, current availability, and the student's deadline. Families should include deadline information with the submission.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent through the Contact page.
